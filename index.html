Our Approach

Accountable, transparent, and built to last.

True North operates as an active owner-operator platform, focused on accountability to all stakeholders — principal investors, co-investors, and lenders. We acquire and operate businesses where we maintain meaningful operational control. We work under written mandates with defined scope, report transparently, and document our decisions and controls to a standard that withstands audit and regulatory review.

Our approach combines operational discipline with independence from conflicting interests. We build management capability in the businesses we operate — leadership, financial control, and governance — rather than creating dependency. This is built for the long term: businesses we operate should become more valuable, more resilient, and more independent over time.

I

Accountable

We operate under clear control structures with defined operational authority and measurable obligations. All stakeholders know exactly what we are responsible for, what we are not, and how we measure success.

II

Transparent

Investors and stakeholders receive complete reporting on business performance, financial position, capital deployment, and any related-party or material arrangements. No surprises, no side arrangements.

III

Disciplined

Decisions, controls, and processes are documented to a standard that withstands audit and regulatory review. We operate as if under constant external scrutiny — always.

IV

Independent

We operate free of conflicting operational interests, maintaining sole focus on the performance and value creation of the businesses we operate. Management decisions serve the businesses and stakeholders — not external advisers or passive management fees.

V

Long-term

We build management capability in the businesses we operate rather than creating dependency. Businesses we operate should become more valuable, more resilient, and more independent over time.

About Us

Independent owner-operator. Accountable. Built for the long term.

True North Management LLC is a Delaware limited liability company, incorporated December 2019, with operations based in Portland, Oregon. We acquire, own, and operate businesses in partnership with investors, maintaining operational control and active management of all investments.

Leadership. K.O. Mathew is Managing Director and principal owner of True North Management LLC. He brings more than two decades of experience founding, building, acquiring, and actively managing operating businesses and education organizations in the United States and internationally. He leads acquisitions and maintains operational control across the True North portfolio.

True North operates as an active owner-operator platform, not as a passive management services firm. We partner with co-investors and lenders but maintain operational control. Engagements are governed by control agreements, disciplined reporting, and operational and financial controls documented to a standard that supports audit, investor, and regulatory review.
